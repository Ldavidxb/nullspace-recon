Accelerated MRI with deep-learning reconstruction raises one question a data-consistency check cannot answer: which parts of the image did the scanner actually measure, and which were supplied by the model?

We have built a verification layer that answers it from the raw data. For Cartesian multi-coil acquisitions it uses an exact eigen-decomposition of the SENSE operator to split every pixel into measured, ill-conditioned and null-space content. It runs a calibrated χ² test on the k-space residuals and writes a hash-bound manifest of the result. Everything runs locally in the browser; no image or raw data leaves the machine.

The short demo below shows why this matters. A lesion of about 7 mm, placed only in the operator’s null space, passes the χ² test because it agrees with the raw data. The provenance map flags it.
